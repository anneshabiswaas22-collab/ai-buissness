# Customer Discovery Interview Guide

**Purpose:** validate wholesale order-intake pain before coding a SaaS product. The same structure can adapt to alternative ideas.

## Consent-first opener

Explain who is conducting the conversation, what will be recorded (if anything), how notes will be used, whether identifiable data will be stored, and that sharing examples is voluntary. **Do not upload or publish any raw order/phone number/photo without explicit permission and approved storage.**

## 20–30 minute interview

1. "Walk me through the last order you received that required manual work."
2. "Where did it arrive: call, text, photo, voice note, staff visit or something else?"
3. "What did you do next, step by step? Where did you get product codes and prices?"
4. "Which part took the longest, and how often does that happen each day?"
5. "How do you handle size/variant confusion, unavailable stock and unit changes?"
6. "What errors happened recently? Who noticed and what did they cost?"
7. "What software or spreadsheet do you use now? What already works well?"
8. "Who would approve or buy a tool that reduces this effort?"
9. "What would stop your staff from using it?"
10. "If a tool prepared a reviewable draft, what exact proof would make it worth paying for?"
11. "Can you show a harmless, synthetic example we can test? Could we run a limited paid pilot?"
12. "Who else performs this workflow that we should speak to?"

## Observer fields

Date, customer segment (no names in public repo), participant role, weekly order volume, baseline minutes/order, current tools, errors, unit/variant patterns, pain frequency, payment authority, integration needs, spoken languages, price reaction, consent status, reasons to reject, follow-up.

## Synthesis

Group by recurring pain, not by most enthusiastic quote. Record negative interviews. Report both denominator and numerator (e.g. 7/20 observed a specific failure). Mark estimates as estimates. Propose a go/no-go with confidence and contrary evidence. Never represent planned interviews as completed.
