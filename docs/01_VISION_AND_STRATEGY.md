# 01 — Vision, Strategy and Non-Goals

## Vision

Build AI-powered software products that a large market **wants to use repeatedly**, not mere demos. Begin with a frequent, painful workflow; prove ROI and distribution; then expand without sacrificing trust, usability or unit economics.

**Founder-confirmed direction:** maximal user adoption / business growth and a detailed executable plan.  
**Proposed operating strategy:** focused B2B wedge for revenue, followed by adjacent workflows and/or a separate higher-reach B2C product.

## Why the lead candidate is B2B

Wholesale order intake can be frequent and financially measurable. Sellers are already working with messages and spreadsheets; replacing entire accounting systems is unnecessary. A workflow assistant can save manual work while leaving pricing, credit and invoice generation in the existing tools. This positioning is a **hypothesis requiring buyer validation**.

## Strategic principles

| Principle | Meaning | Evidence to seek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pain first | Real task currently consumes employee time | Observed weekly order load |
| Narrow wedge | One trade and workflow initially | 3 similar pilot workflows |
| Reviewable AI | Propose actions; humans decide | Low costly-error rate |
| Clear ROI | Time saved exceeds subscription | Measured minutes/order |
| Distribution | Reach buyers via trusted channels | Demo-to-pilot conversion |
| Cost discipline | Minimal variable AI and support cost | Gross margin per account |
| Expand carefully | Broaden only after retention | Renewal and referrals |

## Target users and buyer (hypotheses)

- **Economic buyer:** small/medium electrical wholesaler/distributor owner or operations manager in India.
- **Daily operator:** sales/order desk employee receiving messy inbound requests.
- **Indirect user:** retailer ordering from the distributor; in MVP they need not create an account.
- **Potential future segments:** plumbing/hardware, building materials, other catalogue-heavy distributors.

Validate actual customer sizes, language needs, connectivity, data formats and repeat purchasing before committing.

## Product success definition (proposed)

People repeatedly use the workflow on real orders, safely approve outputs, renew paid subscriptions, recommend it to peers, and see time/quality improvements that outweigh cost. A widely downloaded free app with no sustained usage does not meet this standard.

## Non-goals for MVP

- Fully autonomous buying or payment.
- Replacing Tally or the complete ERP/accounting stack.
- Generic chatbot for every sector.
- Live automatic scraping of messaging accounts.
- Credit decisions, tax or financial advice.
- Enterprise-scale integrations and custom ML training before paid validation.
- A single monolithic app bundling the alternative business concepts.

## Decision gate

Before production build: founder chooses lead opportunity; buyers confirm enough pain; real but appropriately consented sample data is available; trial economics and controls are understood. See [decision log](16_DECISION_LOG_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
