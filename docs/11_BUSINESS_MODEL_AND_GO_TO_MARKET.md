# 11 — Pricing, Distribution and Unit Economics

**Status:** Commercial hypotheses from earlier recommendations; no pricing, customer traction or competitive claims verified.

## Leading B2B model

Test **₹3,000–₹8,000 per wholesale/distribution business per month**, potentially plus one-time onboarding/catalogue setup. These are discussion ranges, **not announced prices**. Validate whether a single owner's avoided labor, faster entry or reduced corrections justify spend.

Avoid selling abstract "AI automation." Demo one troublesome order from the buyer's actual day (with consent), show it as a reviewable draft, then export to familiar billing layout.

## First-market channel hypothesis

Walk through local electrical wholesale markets; personally show operators a fast demo; ask for real (properly anonymized) workflow samples, not compliments. Target **20 discovery interviews** and **3 paid pilots** as milestones, not as accomplished results. Document rejected offers and non-users too.

## Pilot offer draft (requires approval)

- Limited order volume and catalog size; fixed 4-week pilot window.
- Human review mandatory and customer responsible for final billing.
- Clear support channel, data policy and scope limits.
- Defined outcome measure: time from incoming request to verified draft.
- Explicit opt-in, termination and data-deletion terms.

## Unit economics to measure (not predicted)

- Monthly revenue per active paying workspace.
- Onboarding hours × internal cost; support hours per workspace.
- Variable AI transcription/OCR/LLM cost per message and per **approved** order.
- Storage, compute, auth, SMS or messaging charges.
- Gross margin after hosting, model and direct support costs.
- CAC by channel, payback, conversion, churn and customer lifetime (after observing actual retention).

```text
Monthly gross contribution =
  subscription revenue
  - model/OCR/STT costs
  - incremental hosting/storage
  - payment fees
  - direct usage support cost

Break-even acquisition payback (months) =
  paid acquisition + onboarding acquisition expense
  / monthly gross contribution
```

Do not calculate LTV from one-time trial responses.

## Evidence checklist for willingness to pay

- Buyer can name a frequent costly problem.
- Buyer voluntarily shares an example and invests time in trial.
- Buyer commits a paid pilot or signs a meaningful order after terms are understood.
- Actual reviewer workload and mistakes are measured.
- Pilot requests continuing access after first novelty wears off.

## Distribution/expansion

After retention: referrals from wholesale communities, trade associations, local technology partners and targeted case studies with explicit permission. Extend from one vertical only when SKU matching and integrations generalize and sales/support remain manageable.

## Finance discipline

The earlier recommendation included a **₹25,000–₹75,000 validation spend ceiling**; treat it as an exploratory suggestion, not approved expenditure. Track actual spend in a simple ledger and require authorization for paid services or API credentials.
