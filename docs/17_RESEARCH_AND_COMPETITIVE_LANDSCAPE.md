# 17 — Research & Competition Checklist

**Status:** Research framework. This repository does **not** claim verified current competitor features, prices, market shares or user counts.

## Relevant categories to examine

1. **Existing accounting and billing:** e.g. Tally and industry-specific ERP. Determine if order capture is already solved for target shops; don't claim these systems lack features without testing.
2. **Messaging:** WhatsApp / Business / official business API and alternatives. Understand permissions, policies, costs and what can legally be automated.
3. **Catalog / order management SaaS:** Which tools support inbound orders, SKU matching, multi-language and CSV/API export?
4. **Field-service SaaS:** Current dispatch, technician, customer messaging and reporting features.
5. **AI speaking apps / interview simulators:** Existing conversation quality, language support, correction style, free tier, cost.
6. **Returns / logistics platforms:** Exception automation, WMS/e-commerce integrations, support desk overlap.

## Practical differentiation tests

- Does a shop already enter WhatsApp orders into their current billing tool with no friction?
- Is manual SKU matching really the bottleneck, or is fulfillment/inventory availability the problem?
- Would a CSV export fit, or is a specific integration essential to adoption?
- Does voice/OCR help, or is paste-text enough for most orders?
- How often can the user safely trust AI extraction without large manual corrections?
- How fast must the review process be to beat the current workflow?

## Do not infer defensibility from AI alone

Potential defensibility would have to be earned through trusted workflows, customer access, clean catalogues, human-reviewed aliases, integrations, support quality and actual retention. None is proven yet.
