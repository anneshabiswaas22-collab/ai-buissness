# 02 — AI Business Opportunity Portfolio

**Source context:** assistant recommendation from 2026-10-28; **not** a validated market survey. Prices are test hypotheses in INR per customer per month.

| Priority | Opportunity | Core pain solved | Narrow starting user | Price hypothesis | Main uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AI Order Assistant | Unstructured orders need manual SKU matching and data entry | Electrical wholesaler order desk | ₹3,000–₹8,000 + possible setup | Will pay for reliable review-first automation? |
| 2 | Field service operations | Dispatch and technician updates scattered across calls/chats | Local maintenance/service operator | ₹2,000–₹6,000 | Sufficient differentiation from field-service SaaS? |
| 3 | AI job-focused English/interview coach | Learners need realistic speaking practice and corrective feedback | Role-specific job seeker; Hindi/Bengali support | ₹199–₹499 | Consumer acquisition, habit retention, inference economics |
| 4 | E-commerce delivery / returns queue | Exceptions require repeated manual coordination | Merchant with sizable shipment volume | ₹5,000–₹15,000 | Integration complexity and operational access |

## Decision matrix (illustrative judgment, not market data)

Use a 1–5 score only **after customer interviews**. Weight: pain 25%, reachability of first customers 20%, repeat usage 20%, willingness to pay 20%, technical / regulatory simplicity 15%. Do not substitute arbitrary scores for evidence.

### Idea 1 — AI Order Assistant (lead recommendation)

- Input: pasted order text / uploaded images / audio notes, plus a seller-owned catalogue.
- Output: candidate line items with SKU, unit, quantity, ambiguity flags and staff-reviewed CSV handoff.
- Wedge: interpretation of incoming requests, not invoice creation.
- Expansion: reorder memory, multilingual intake, recurring item aliases, approved ERP connectors.
- Risk: SKU mix-ups cause real money loss; safety and review are essential.

### Idea 2 — Service operations

- Capture service jobs, schedule technician, communicate status, store service history, remind for maintenance.
- Differentiate by efficient workflow, optional voice intake, local language and affordable setup.
- Avoid a broad CRM from day one.

### Idea 3 — AI English speaking/interview practice

- Real-time or turn-based conversational role-play with corrective feedback and progress.
- Initial job-focused scenarios; Hindi/Bengali support where requested.
- Coach mode and conversation mode can serve different learning preferences.
- Need low latency, controllable AI spend, pedagogy, privacy and demonstrable retention.
- May reach more individual users but has a harder monetization and acquisition problem.

### Idea 4 — E-commerce delivery/returns assistant

- Consolidate return or shipment exceptions, triage, assignment, communication draft and resolution queue.
- Human approval for messages/refunds; integrations require permissions and contracts.
- Strong ROI potential but operational and privacy complexity.

## Validation interview questions shared across ideas

1. Tell me about the *last* time this problem happened. Show the process if you can.
2. How often and how long does it take? Who does the work?
3. What does an error cost? How is it detected?
4. What tools do you use now, and why?
5. Who has purchase authority? What budget and approval process exists?
6. Would you trial an assisted workflow? What would make it unacceptable?
7. What data and permissions would a vendor need?
8. Would you pay for a limited pilot now? At what proof threshold?

## Portfolio selection rule

Select ONE first MVP. The founder has not approved the selection. Record interview evidence, expected costs, willingness to pay, and distribution before choosing. Keep other briefs as **future options**, not simultaneous commitments.
